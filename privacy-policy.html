Dawarx POS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. About Dawarx POS

Dawarx POS is a pharmacy point-of-sale and inventory management application designed to assist with pharmacy business operations.

2. Google Services

Dawarx POS may connect to Google services when the user chooses to enable Google integration.

The application may use Google Sheets to synchronize information required for its configured pharmacy features and Google Drive to store database backup files created by the application.

3. Google Account Information

When Google integration is enabled, Dawarx POS receives the authorization information necessary to access the Google services that the user has approved.

Google account credentials such as the user's Google password are not accessed or stored by Dawarx POS.

4. Database Backups

When Google Drive backup is enabled, Dawarx POS may create compressed database backup files in the user's Google Drive.

Backup files are created for the purpose of protecting the user's Dawarx POS data and enabling restoration of the application data.

5. Data Control

The user controls whether Google integration is enabled. The user may disconnect the Google account from Dawarx POS.

Disconnecting Google integration does not delete the user's local Dawarx POS database.

6. Data Sharing

Dawarx POS does not sell the user's data. Google services are used only when the user has enabled the corresponding integration.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy or Dawarx POS, please contact the Dawarx POS administrator.
